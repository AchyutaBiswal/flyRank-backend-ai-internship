### Image

Cyberpunk futuristic city with robots, flying cars, neon lights, and glowing servers.

Although visually impressive, the image distracted from my backend engineering portfolio and did not match the clean, minimal design language established by my identity kit. I chose to prioritize clarity, professionalism, and consistency over visual complexity.

### Real Images
